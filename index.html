About Anvi

Family-run taste of Jharkhand, served with hygiene & heart

Founded in Bistupur, Jamshedpur in 2010, Anvi Caterers began as a small family kitchen for pujas and house parties. Today, we cater everything from Tata Steel corporate lunches to destination weddings at Dalma foothills.

Our promise is simple: great taste, on-time service, spotless hygiene, and separate pure-veg and non-veg kitchens that respect every tradition. Chefs from Punjab, UP, Bengal and South India cook authentic recipes with locally sourced Jharkhand ingredients.
